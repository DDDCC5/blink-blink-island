# Voice script / 双语配音稿

Filename convention: voice_zh_<id>.wav and voice_en_<id>.wav; voice recordings are **not included**. Use licensed narration or a voice synthesis tool with appropriate rights. Never play narration simultaneously in both languages.

| id | 中文 | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| intro_blink | 请完整闭上眼睛，轻轻收紧眼睑，保持三秒。不要按压眼球。 | Close your eyes fully and gently squeeze your eyelids for three seconds. Do not press on your eyes. |
| closed | 已闭眼，保持一会儿。 | Eyes closed. Hold gently. |
| count_3 | 三。 | Three. |
| count_2 | 二。 | Two. |
| count_1 | 一。 | One. |
| open | 现在可以睁开眼睛。 | You can open your eyes now. |
| butterfly | 保持头部自然稳定，用视线慢慢跟随蝴蝶。可以自然眨眼。 | Keep your head comfortably steady and follow the butterfly with your eyes. Blink naturally. |
| gaze_left_right | 看向左边，再看向右边，然后回到左边。 | Look left, then right, then back left. |
| gaze_up_down | 看向上方，再看向下方，然后回到上方。 | Look up, then down, then back up. |
| gaze_clockwise | 顺时针看完一整圈。 | Follow one full clockwise circle. |
| gaze_counter | 逆时针看完一整圈。 | Follow one full counterclockwise circle. |
| incomplete | 这次动作还没有完成，可以重试或跳过。 | This move was not completed. Retry or skip. |
| tracking_paused | 训练已暂停，请检查设备的佩戴或眼动追踪状态。 | Session paused. Check your headset fit or eye tracking. |
| finish | 这次休息训练完成了。 | Your break is complete. |
| discomfort | 如果感到疼痛、眩晕或不适，请停止并摘下头显休息。 | If you feel pain, dizziness, or discomfort, stop and remove the headset to rest. |

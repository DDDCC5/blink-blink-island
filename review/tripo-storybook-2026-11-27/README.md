# Tripo nature-storybook redesign

User explicitly selected natural storybook: cream UI, soft vegetation and butterfly.

Preserve the running 1.0.5 app until the new design and real generated models are reviewed. This is a new production package, not a claim that Tripo generates functioning UI.

Active assets: IslandBase (1), CanopyTree (2), FlowerPatch (3), PebbleCluster (2), ButterflyAnatomical (1 complete mesh). Old ButterflyBody/ButterflyWing are retained only as rejected history. Their mirrored assembly and geometry-trim approach are no longer used.

Palette: cream #F6F5E9, ink #254E43, sage #8AC0A0, mint #D7EDE4, teal #398F99. Soft matte hand-painted surface; no hard circular backing, flashing lights or competing central scenery.

UI page inventory: welcome, home, pre-training plan/pace setup, three training modes with permission/preparing/active/paused/retry states, summary, records, seven-day statistics, settings, help and delete confirmation. Native SpatialUI remains responsible for interaction, readable text and controls. Left fixed panel; right island; center training target.
